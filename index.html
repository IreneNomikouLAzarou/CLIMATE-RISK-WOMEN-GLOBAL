CLIMATE RISK WOMEN GLOBAL is a global network of women and women-identified researchers and professionals. We conduct interdisciplinary research on the impacts of climate risk across scientific, economic, and social systems translating findings into evidence-based policy and practice.

Our mission

Representation is not symbolic. It is a precondition for research that works.

We empower women and women-identified professionals through mentorship, foster research collaboration across disciplines, and create a space where diverse ideas — and the people behind them — can thrive.

Our work connects climate science, finance, insurance, urban systems, cultural heritage, aquaculture, AI, public health, neuroscience and the humanities, translating evidence into policy influence and on-the-ground action.

Who we are

An interdisciplinary network with a single purpose.

Our story

2024 to 2026: from an informal collaboration to an institution.

CRWG began in 2024 as an informal collaboration of women researchers and professionals working on climate risk and resilience. In 2026 it was formally established as a non-profit organization (AMKE), to institutionalize and scale its mission.

Our approach is deliberately interdisciplinary. We bring together expertise that rarely sits at the same table — climate science, finance, insurance, urban systems, cultural heritage, aquaculture, AI, public health, neuroscience and the humanities — to address how climate risk is experienced, measured and mitigated in the real world.

“We believe that representation is not a symbolic goal but a precondition for research that actually works — and that diverse teams produce more robust, more equitable climate solutions.”
